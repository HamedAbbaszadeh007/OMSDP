#  Core Domains

## Purpose

This document identifies the core business domains of OMSDP.

Each domain represents a major responsibility of the platform and serves as the foundation for the domain model, database design, APIs, and software architecture.

---

## Core Domains

### Spatial Data Management
Management of vector, raster, and 3D spatial data, including layers, features, metadata, and map services.

### User & Identity Management
Authentication, authorization, user profiles, roles, permissions, and security.

### Municipal Asset Management
Management of municipal assets such as roads, buildings, parks, utilities, and public facilities.

### Spatial Analysis
Geospatial analysis, spatial queries, statistics, modeling, and decision support.

### Data Integration
Integration with external systems through APIs, data exchange, and interoperability standards.

### Workflow & Business Processes
Management of spatial workflows, review processes, approvals, and task assignments.

### AI & Spatial Intelligence
AI-assisted spatial analysis, anomaly detection, prediction, natural language GIS, and intelligent decision support.

### Reporting & Dashboards
Generation of reports, dashboards, KPIs, charts, and spatial summaries.

### Administration
System configuration, monitoring, auditing, logging, backup, and maintenance.
